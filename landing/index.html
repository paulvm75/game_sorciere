A world on the edge.

An ancient veil is breaking, exposing a hidden supernatural world. Drawn together by the same forbidden power, Maëlle and Kaen must uncover its origins before the divide disappears forever.

Paris after midnight.

Rooftops, the Seine and the iron tower. Something is hunting up there, and Maëlle has a broom.

II · CALAIS

A door on the sand.

Lyra sings, and the light opens a way across the Channel.

III · LONDON

Kaen. Or one of him.

The Master of Illusions waits in the rain. Nobody knows which face is real.

IV · MONT-SAINT-MICHEL

The tide remembers.

An abbey on a rock in the sea, and a queen who will not stay drowned.
