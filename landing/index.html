The story so far

Eighteen chapters. Four acts.

It starts with a masked fox on the rooftops of Paris. It ends, for now, on a rock in the sea, with a dead queen's true name.

Chapters I - VIII
I
Paris, after midnight

The Master of Illusions

Midnight, and something is hunting the rooftops. Maëlle chases Hakuko, the Masked Fox, across the zinc roofs and into a war she never chose: a frost giant at the Eiffel Tower, a grinning cat demon in the Seine, Morwen, Queen of the Abyss, and at the end of it all Kaen Kuroshiba, the Master of Illusions.

Chapters IX - XI
II
Calais · the Channel · London

The Crossing

Kaen's prison breaks. His sister Lyra opens a door to Calais, and Maëlle flies the cursed Channel through bats, winged jack-o'-lanterns and gargoyles. Greyfang waits on the clock tower. At the Tower of London the Warden takes a body, and Kaen comes home as a ghost riding her broom.

Chapters XII - XVI
III
London · Westminster

The Hollow Crown

Ash from the Trocadéro, six ravens and a heartbeat give Kaen his body back. Then the truth about the Crown: a brother made of smoke in a cloaked pub, an abbey full of wings, Gog and Magog, and a chair where something has been waiting. Every face it ever wore. Then yours.

Chapters XVII - XVIII
IV
The Sorbonne · Mont-Saint-Michel

The Tide Door

Lyra opens a door home. Under the Sorbonne someone still remembers Morwen, and a baptism record points to Mont-Saint-Michel. Her true name is in the parish register, unless the Crown got there first. Below the abbey, the Drowned Bell is still ringing.
